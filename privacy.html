Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

FireDash is an iOS app that lets you administer Firebase projects you already own, from an iPhone or iPad. This policy explains exactly what it touches and what it does not.

The short version. FireDash has no servers of its own. It runs entirely on your device and talks directly to Google's APIs using your own Google sign-in. Your Firebase data is never sent anywhere except between your device and Google. We never see it.

1. Who we are

FireDash is developed by Kelvin Tan, an independent app developer. For any privacy question, contact kelvin.vins@gmail.com.

FireDash is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Google LLC.

2. Signing in with Google

To administer your Firebase projects, FireDash asks you to sign in with Google. It requests narrow, per-service permissions rather than blanket access to your Google Cloud account. Every permission it can ask for is listed below.

PermissionUsed for
auth/firebaseListing the Firebase projects you own, and reading and publishing security rules, Remote Config, Hosting releases and App Check settings
auth/datastoreReading and editing your Firestore documents and indexes
auth/identitytoolkitViewing and managing the users of your own apps
auth/firebase.databaseReading and editing your Realtime Database data and rules
auth/devstorage.read_writeBrowsing, downloading, uploading and deleting your Cloud Storage files
auth/cloudfunctionsListing and inspecting your Cloud Functions
auth/logging.readReading the logs your Cloud Functions produce
auth/firebase.messagingSending a push message you compose, to a target you enter
auth/analytics.readonlyReading the Google Analytics property linked to your project
auth/monitoring.readReading usage counts — Firestore operations, egress, function errors and sign-ins — for the Usage screen and for usage alerts

FireDash does not request cloud-platform, which would grant it read and write access to every Google Cloud resource you own. Each permission above covers one service, so declining one leaves the rest working.

Your Google account credentials are never seen by FireDash. Sign-in is handled by Google's own SDK, which returns an access token. That token is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and is used only to call Google's APIs on your behalf. Signing out removes it.

Limited Use disclosure

FireDash's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the features described on this page, is never sold, is never used for advertising, and is never transferred to third parties except as required to provide those features or to comply with the law.

3. Your Firebase project data

When you browse a collection, edit a document, publish rules or change a config value, that request goes directly from your device to Google's servers. It does not pass through any server operated by FireDash, because there isn't one.

  • Your project data is not copied to any FireDash server.
  • Your project data is not shared with any third party.
  • Data fetched for display is held in memory and cleared when you switch projects or sign out.
  • FireDash remembers only which project you last had selected, stored locally on your device.

4. Analytics

FireDash records a small amount of anonymous usage data so we can tell which features are worth continuing to build. This is deliberately limited to which kind of screen was opened and whether an action succeeded.

No identifiers from your projects are ever recorded. Analytics events carry only fixed values from a predefined list — for example service_opened with service: firestore. The app is built so that project IDs, collection names, document IDs, field values, file paths and email addresses cannot be included in an analytics event, even accidentally.

This data is processed by Google Analytics for Firebase. One event, alerts_fired, carries a count of how many usage alerts triggered on a check — a number, never which project or which metric.

5. What we never collect

  • The contents of your Firebase data
  • Your Google account password
  • Your contacts, photos, location, or device identifiers for advertising
  • Any personal information about your app's users

6. Data retention and deletion

Because FireDash stores nothing on a server, there is no account to delete. Removing the app from your device removes the stored access token and the locally remembered project selection. You can also revoke FireDash's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

7. Children

FireDash is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
