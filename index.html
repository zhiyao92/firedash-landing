Something broke while you were out. FireDash lets you look at your Firestore data, fix a document, flip a Remote Config value or unblock a user — without opening a laptop.

What it does

Sign in with Google, pick any Firebase project you own, and work with it directly.

Firestore

Browse collections and documents, filter and order with a query builder, and edit fields with full type support — strings, numbers, timestamps, geopoints, references, maps and arrays.

Security rules

Read the live ruleset, edit it, compile it against Firebase's own validator, and publish. FireDash refuses to publish rules that don't compile.

Remote Config

Change how your shipped app behaves without a release. Edits are version-guarded, so you can't silently overwrite a change someone else published.

Authentication

Find a user by email, see whether they're disabled, flip that, and edit custom claims. The "I can't log in" email, answered from a phone.

Built to not ruin your day

A console on a phone is one mis-tap from a production incident. FireDash is designed around that.

  • Every write shows a diff first. Not "Are you sure?" — the actual before and after, field by field.
  • Deletes make you type the name. No swipe-to-delete on production data, ever.
  • Nothing loads everything. Every list is paged with an explicit limit, because every document read is billed to you.
  • Broken rules are refused. A ruleset that doesn't compile can lock every app out of a project at once.
  • Usage alerts before the bill. FireDash watches your Firestore reads, egress, function errors and sign-ins, and tells you when today looks nothing like a normal day.

Your data stays yours

FireDash has no servers. It talks directly from your device to Google's APIs using your own Google sign-in, and it asks only for the permissions each screen needs, when you first open that screen. Your Firebase data is never copied anywhere else.
